An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

Open your launcher and press **Update** (then Play). It rebuilds the game with everything below. Download the new
`PGR2Launcher.exe` here if you are installing fresh, or to use a media patched disc image (see below).

## What's new since 1.0.2

- **Paris (Xbox Live DLC) races without crashing.** Paris needs more room in some of the game's city tables than the
  disc version has (the title update made them bigger). The overflow made the game crash a second after Paris loaded,
  left the road surface missing on the Pont d'Iéna, and stopped races with "There's a problem with the disc" (most
  often just after the start line of L'Arc de Triomphe). The extra entries are now kept aside.
- **The radio follows the Music volume** (Options > Sound / Music). It played at full volume whatever the setting.
- **The DLC packs are checked at start:** with the DLC on, the log says whether each pack's files match the pack's own
  list (`content pack ...: all 20 files match`) and names any damaged file to copy again.
- **Pedal boxes on any axes:** pedals that report Z, Z rotation or sliders (Leo Bodnar BU0836 and similar) now bind in
  **F1 > Wheel**. Before, only a device's first few axes could be bound. The log lists each device's axes.
- **Ultrawide:** the rear view mirror (bumper and hood views) stays inside its frame on screens wider than 16:9,
  instead of stretching into the HUD.
- **Profiles from a real Xbox:** a profile copied off a console (an Action Replay save, for example) no longer shows as
  damaged. It is signed for the PC the next time it saves.
- **Media patched disc images** (made to run from a modded console's hard disk) are accepted: they change one byte of
  start-up code the port replaces anyway. This needs the new `PGR2Launcher.exe` below.
- A rarely used menu callback that was never compiled is now included (opening its screen would have crashed).

## New install

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own (for example `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**
   (about 1.4 GB of portable build tools, 30 to 90 minutes the first time, about 15 GB free needed; nothing is
   installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).
Please report problems with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or `PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
