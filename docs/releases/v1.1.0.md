An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

Download the new **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in place of your old one (same folder: it keeps your build,
tools and saves), then press **Update** and Play. The new launcher is needed for **Your music**; Update alone brings
everything else.

## What's new since 1.0.3

### Your music in the game
PGR2 could always play custom soundtracks that an Xbox ripped from your CDs. The launcher now makes them from a
folder of your own music:
- New launcher section **4  Your music**: *Choose a music folder...* Each folder inside becomes a soundtrack (an
  artist's albums can sit in one), and songs directly in the chosen folder make one more.
- MP3, FLAC, OGG, Opus, M4A, AAC, WAV, WMA and AIFF are converted for the game with FFmpeg, which the launcher
  downloads the first time (about 90 MB into its tools folder, nothing installed). Song names come from their tags.
- *Update my music* after adding or changing songs: only those are converted.
- In the game: Options > Sound / Music > **PGR 2 Custom Soundtrack** > Add/Remove Songs (the triggers step to your
  soundtracks, after the game's own), then choose the custom soundtrack in a race's pause menu (Radio/CD Options).
- A MUSIC folder copied from a real Xbox works as it is.

### Sharp text and HUD
The menus', HUD's and text's textures are enlarged two or four times with sharp edges, so lettering, the
speedometer and gauges stay crisp at high resolutions instead of looking stepped and soft. On by default; F1 >
Graphics > **Sharp text and HUD** turns it off. Painted pictures only get smoother, not more detailed.

### Fixes
- **Nürburgring's sky** (with the DLC on, reached through the TVR Cerbera Speed 12's Car Challenge): the sky was black
  in the flyover and white with brown bars that flashed while driving. The sky dome and far hills lie past the
  game's far plane, which the Xbox keeps and PC GPUs were cutting off; they are drawn again.
- **Fewer stutters:** compiled shaders are now kept on disk (`shadercache.bin` beside the game), so the pauses when a
  new area's shaders were first needed happen once, not every time you play.
- **Quit game** button at the top right of the settings window (F1, or Back + Start), which asks first; Alt+F4 also
  closes the game.
- More reliable memory use when large areas load, and several rendering fixes shared with the other ports built on
  the same toolkit (ultrawide 2D, depth buffer reads, blend modes).

## New install

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own (for example `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**
   (about 1.4 GB of portable build tools, 30 to 90 minutes the first time, about 15 GB free needed; nothing is
   installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).
Please report problems with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or `PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
