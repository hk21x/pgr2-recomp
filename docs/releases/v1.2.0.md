An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

Download the new **PGR2Launcher.exe** below **by hand this one time** and put it in place of your old one (same
folder: it keeps your build, tools, saves and settings), then press **Update** and Play.

From this launcher on, updates come to you: when a new release is out, the launcher offers it at the top of its
window (**Update the launcher**, **What's new**, **Later**). It downloads the new launcher, checks it against the
SHA-256 GitHub lists, swaps it in and restarts, then offers to rebuild the game for that version, keeping your saves
and settings. Launchers before this one cannot offer updates, which is why this one is downloaded by hand.

## What's new since 1.1.0

### PC Settings in the game's own menus
Options > General Options > **PC Settings** is now a page of the game's own options, changed with left and right like
the rest and applied at once: render scale, anti-aliasing, texture filtering, sharp text and HUD, draw distance,
field of view, the race HUD and the frame counter. Its last row, **More Settings**, opens the settings window (F1)
for display, controls, the wheel and everything else. Both save to the same settings file.

### Steering wheels
- **No more dead zone and bent steering:** the game is now told that controller 1 is a steering wheel when your
  steering is bound in F1 > Wheel, as an Xbox wheel did. It then takes your steering as it comes, instead of adding
  a gamepad's dead zone and stick correction (a large dead zone, then a non-linear response). The Wheel tab's
  rotation, steering lock and dead zone are now the whole response; try the dead zone at 0.
- **The d-pad from buttons:** F1 > Wheel > Buttons has D-pad Up, Down, Left and Right, so a wheel without a hat (or a
  button box) can work the menus. A hat on the steering device still works as the d-pad as well.

### Fixes
- **The rear view mirror on ultrawide screens:** its picture ran past its frame on both sides at 21:9 and 32:9; the
  two now match.
- **The launcher and long folder paths:** a build started from a deeply nested folder stopped while unpacking the
  Windows SDK, with nothing in the log. The launcher now says when its folder's path is too long and asks for a short
  one (such as `C:\Games\PGR2`), and logs why any build stopped.
- Fixes shared with the other ports built on the same toolkit: the game restarts into itself without a suspended
  start (which antivirus tools can mistake for something suspicious), the renderer's own buffers no longer come out
  of the game's memory, and rendering fixes (gamma, vertex data the GPU drew).

## New install

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
   `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**
   (about 1.4 GB of portable build tools, 30 to 90 minutes the first time, about 15 GB free needed; nothing is
   installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings; Options > General Options > PC Settings has the
   picture settings.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).
Please report problems with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or `PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
