An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.2.0:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher**, then
**Update** to rebuild the game, keeping your saves and settings, then Play.

**From an older version:** download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below by hand and put it in place of your old one (same
folder: it keeps your build, tools, saves and settings), then press **Update** and Play. It offers updates itself from
then on.

## What's new since 1.2.0

### Choose the game's language
The game can now run in any of the eight languages on your disc: English, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Italian,
Korean and Chinese. On an Xbox the console's dashboard chose; now it's the **Language** row in Options > General
Options > PC Settings, or F1 > Game.

- It applies at the next start, which copies the game's files again, as the first start did (a couple of minutes of
  movies). Starting again in the same language does not repeat this.
- Japanese keeps the disc's own English wording in parts of the race HUD ("Lap Time").
- The PC Settings rows and the F1 window are in English whichever language you choose.

### Other changes
- The launcher writes what its update check found to `launcher.log`, which helps if an update isn't offered.
- Fixes shared with the other ports built on the same toolkit.

## New install

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
   `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**
   (about 1.4 GB of portable build tools, 30 to 90 minutes the first time, about 15 GB free needed; nothing is
   installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings; Options > General Options > PC Settings has the
   picture settings and the language.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).
Help and reports: the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or
`PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
