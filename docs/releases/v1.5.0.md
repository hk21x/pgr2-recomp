An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.2.0 or later:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher**, then
**Update** to rebuild the game, keeping your saves and settings, then Play.

**From an older version:** download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below by hand and put it in place of your old one (same
folder: it keeps your build, tools, saves and settings), then press **Update** and Play. It offers updates itself from
then on.

## What's new since 1.4.0

### Races above 30 frames a second
- **Race Frame Rate:** 30 as on the console, 60, 90, 120 or 144, in Recomp and F1 > Game. It applies at the next start,
  and the physics are unchanged. 30 stays the default.
- 60 holds on a fast CPU. Above that, busy streets dip on most CPUs, and a variable refresh display hides dips best.
  Replays can dip at render scale 200%.
- A dip no longer puts the race in slow motion: game time now follows the clock.
- Split-screen above 30 has not been tested yet. Reports are welcome.

### Recomp replaces PC Settings
- **Options > General Options > Recomp** now holds the settings in sections: Picture, Performance, Display, Game,
  Controls, Wheel, Bindings and More, with Quit Game. It also opens from the **pause menu**, offline and outside replays.
- The **F1 settings window** has a new look with sections and full-width rows, and works well with a controller.

### More picture settings
- **SMAA**, **Smooth Alpha Edges** (leaves and other cut-out shapes, with MSAA on), **Texture Filtering** up to 16x and a
  **Sharp scaling filter**. Texture Filtering 16x makes the largest difference: the road stays sharp into the distance.
- **Enhanced World Textures** is off by default and best left off. It adds little and can cause hitches of a quarter of
  a second.
- A good place to start on a capable PC: render scale 200%, MSAA 4x, Smooth Alpha Edges, Texture Filtering 16x,
  sharpening 50%. The [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#graphics-settings) compares them on screenshots.

### Smaller changes
- **F12** saves a full resolution PNG in the `screenshots` folder beside the game.
- The mouse pointer no longer sits over the game.
- The launcher's build tools now use a different edition of Git, which fixes a first build that failed on one player's
  PC with error `0xC0000142`.

## New install

1. Download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
   `C:\Games\PGR2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**
   (about 1.4 GB of portable build tools, 30 to 90 minutes the first time, about 3 GB free needed, plus room for your copy of the game; nothing is
   installed in Windows, no admin rights).
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings; Options > General Options > Recomp has them
   too.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp#readme).
Help and reports: the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc), with `PGR2\play\logs\pgr2.log` (the game) or
`PGR2\launcher.log` (building).
