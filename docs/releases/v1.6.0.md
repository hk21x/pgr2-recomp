An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.2.0 or later:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher**. The
new launcher shows **A faster way to play**: press **Update**. Your game keeps playing until the new one is ready, and
your saves, settings and music are kept. Afterwards, **Manage > Remove the old build tools** frees about 2 GB.

**From an older version:** download **PGR2Launcher.exe** below by hand and put it in place of your old one, in the same
folder, then press **Update**.

## What's new since 1.5.1

### Installing takes a minute, not half an hour
- The launcher no longer downloads 1.4 GB of build tools or builds everything from source. It downloads this release's
  package (about 70 MB: the program and a compiler, no game code), and your PC builds only the game's code from your
  disc. On a fast 16 thread PC that took about 20 seconds; it depends on your PC and connection.
- No Microsoft license step, no Git, no CMake.
- Updates are built beside the game and only replace it once ready: a failed or stopped update leaves the game as it was.

### A new launcher
- The classic look of the F1 settings window: pages for **Game**, **Your music**, **Manage** and **Log**, the steps as a
  list, and the progress in the footer on every page.
- **Manage** shows the install, opens its folders, frees space, and builds the game again if it will not start.

### Smaller changes
- **Enhanced World Textures** loads its pictures a share a frame, aimed at its hitches. Not yet measured on a PC: it
  stays off by default.

## Files

- **PGR2Launcher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **PGR2-package.zip** and **PGR2-package.zip.json**: the package the launcher downloads, and its size and checksum.
  You do not need to download or unzip them.
