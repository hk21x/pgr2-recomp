An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.6.0:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher** if it asks,
then **Update**. Your saves, settings and music are kept.

**From 1.2.0 to 1.5.1:** press **Update the launcher**, then **Update**, as described in the
[1.6.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/releases/tag/v1.6.0).

## What's new since 1.6.0

### Ultrawide fills the screen by default ([#33](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/33))
- **Aspect Ratio: From Screen**, the default, now widens the view on an ultrawide screen (21:9 and wider) instead of
  showing 16:9. The HUD stays in a 16:9 area in the middle. Before, only **Fill Screen** widened it.
- Switching between **16:9** and **Fill Screen** applies at once, without stretching the picture. Changing to or from
  **4:3** still needs a restart, as the game picks its TV mode when it starts.

### Fixes
- **Speed camera photos** show your car again, on the result page and in Options > Profile > Speed Camera. They were a
  grey or black box. ([#30](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/30))
- **Washington's Capitol dome** no longer shows the sky through it with **Smooth Alpha Edges** on.
  ([#28](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/28))
- **The intro movies** keep their 4:3 shape, with black bars at the sides, instead of being stretched across a wide
  screen. The title screen is unchanged. ([#26](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/26))
- **Recomp in the pause menu** keeps the pause menu's darkened background, so its text is readable over a bright sky.
  ([#32](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/32))

### Known
- Races at 120 frames a second dip to about 90 in busy parts of a track on most PCs: the renderer's cost per draw. 60
  holds; a variable refresh display (FreeSync, G-Sync) hides the dips best. Work on this is next.

## Files

- **PGR2Launcher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **PGR2-package.zip** and **PGR2-package.zip.json**: the package the launcher downloads, and its size and checksum.
  You do not need to download or unzip them.
