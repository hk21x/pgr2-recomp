An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.6.0 or 1.6.1:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher** if
it asks, then **Update**. Your saves, settings and music are kept.

**From 1.2.0 to 1.5.1:** press **Update the launcher**, then **Update**, as described in the
[1.6.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/releases/tag/v1.6.0).

## What's new since 1.6.1

### Fixes
- **Menu transitions** fill the whole screen again on 16:9 and wider screens. Since 1.6.1 the fades between menu pages
  only covered the 4:3 middle, leaving the edges unchanged. The intro movies still keep their 4:3 shape.
  ([#36](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/36))
- **Reflections cost less.** The cars' reflections reuse their working textures instead of making new ones every frame,
  which lifts busy racing at high frame rates (about 85 to 110 frames a second on a test PC at 120) and removes a
  cause of stutter.

### Known
- Races at 120 frames a second still dip in the busiest parts of a track. A larger renderer change that spreads its
  work over more CPU cores is being tested for the next release.

## Files

- **PGR2Launcher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **PGR2-package.zip** and **PGR2-package.zip.json**: the package the launcher downloads, and its size and checksum.
  You do not need to download or unzip them.
