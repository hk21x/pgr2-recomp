An update to the unofficial native PC port of Project Gotham Racing 2 (original Xbox, PAL and NTSC).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.6.0 to 1.6.3:** the launcher offers this release at the top of its window. Press **Update the launcher** if
it asks, then **Update**. Your saves, settings and music are kept.

**From 1.2.0 to 1.5.1:** press **Update the launcher**, then **Update**, as described in the
[1.6.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/releases/tag/v1.6.0).

## What's new since 1.6.3

### New
- **CRT look** (F1 > Graphics, 0 to 100%): scanlines and a CRT's red, green and blue stripes over the picture, HUD
  included, with no second app. The lines are thin on dark parts and fuller on bright ones, as on a real tube, and
  each line is a whole number of screen pixels, so they stay even. It needs a picture 720 pixels tall or more for the
  lines, and looks best at 1440p and above. ([#43](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/43))
- **A dead zone and a sensitivity for each stick** (F1 > Controls). The dead zone is how far a stick moves before it
  counts; the sensitivity scales how far it turns. The defaults leave the sticks as before.
- **Start the game straight from the launcher:** a box under Play, "Start the game when the launcher opens, if
  nothing needs updating", or `--play` on a shortcut (handy for Steam Big Picture). Hold Shift as the launcher opens,
  or press **Stay in the launcher**, to stay in it.

### Fixes
- **Enhanced world textures:** some of Florence's buildings lost whole walls, so the sky showed through them, as at
  the Piazza della Repubblica arch. The walls now stay.
  ([#40](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/40))
- **The launcher no longer needs two copies of the game's `.iso`.** Choosing an image that is already in the
  launcher's `play\assets` folder uses it where it is. ([#39](https://github.com/hk21x/pgr2-recomp/issues/39))
- **Updates and first builds** no longer stop with "Access is denied" when an antivirus scan holds a new folder for a
  moment: the launcher keeps trying for up to two minutes.

## Files

- **PGR2Launcher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **PGR2-package.zip** and **PGR2-package.zip.json**: the package the launcher downloads, and its size and checksum.
  You do not need to download or unzip them.
